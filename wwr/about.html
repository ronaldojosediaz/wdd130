History

Blue River Rafting was founded in 1998 by two friends who shared a love for the river and a passion for outdoor adventure. What began as a single raft and a small group of neighbors has grown into one of the region's most trusted rafting companies. Over the years, our experienced guides have led thousands of families, friends, and first-time adventurers safely through the rapids. Today, we continue to protect the river we love while offering unforgettable trips for every skill level.
